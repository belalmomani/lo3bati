Game Icons (game-icons.net)
===========================

The icons of the 'Game' set (swords, shields, potions, monsters, ...) come from Game Icons, https://game-icons.net,
icons by Lorc, Delapouite & contributors, licensed under Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 (CC BY 3.0).

  name:    Game Icons

  license: CC BY 3.0 (CC-BY-3.0) https://github.com/game-icons/icons/blob/master/license.txt

Changes made by Star2D (adaptation notice required by CC BY 3.0): only a selection of the icons is included; the SVG path
data is kept, with numbers rounded to two decimals; the shapes are drawn as plain single-colour PNG images (white by default,
or tinted with the colour chosen when importing), optionally on a coloured badge or with a shadow.

You may use these icons in your games, including commercial ones, as long as you give credit, for example in the credits
screen: 'Icons by Lorc, Delapouite & contributors from game-icons.net, CC BY 3.0'.
